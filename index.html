Our story

Started small.
Stayed curious.

2016

Three engineers, one rented desk

Aorue began as a weekend experiment between three friends who kept finding the same broken problem in every product they touched: great ideas, terrible foundations.

2019

First million users served

A client platform we architected crossed one million monthly users without a single rewrite. Word spread. The team grew to twelve.

2022

The AI division opens

We started shipping production ML systems — recommendation engines, document intelligence, and real-time fraud detection.

2025

Forty-two people, eighteen countries

A distributed studio with a single standard: if we build it, it has to outlive us. Today we partner with teams on four continents.
